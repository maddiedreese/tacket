## Install

1. Download `Tacket.dmg`.
2. Open it and drag Tacket into Applications.
3. Open Tacket.
4. In Tacket, go to **Settings** and click **Install Local Connector**.
5. Add the Chrome extension:
   https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tacket/cbpgfpcajomllnfoigagibafblmnbbdh

The Mac app stores and searches your saved chats locally. The Chrome extension saves browser chats from ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini into the app.

## Privacy

Tacket runs locally on your Mac. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no Tacket server that receives your chats.

## Verify

Download `SHA256SUMS` with the release assets to verify `Tacket.dmg` and `tacket-chrome-extension.zip`.
