Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Local-only by design

Tacket captures AI chat threads only when you explicitly click Capture This Thread. Captured data is sent to the local Tacket app through Chrome Native Messaging and written to local files on your Mac.

What Tacket does not collect

  • No analytics
  • No telemetry
  • No remote crash reports
  • No background chat collection
  • No model/API calls
  • No Tacket backend account

Browser permissions

The Chrome extension requests access only to supported AI chat domains and only uses that access after you click the extension capture button. The popup refuses capture outside ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini hosts.

Local files

Captured `.tacket` bundles may contain private conversation text, code, images, and attachment references. You control where those files are stored and whether they are committed to git or shared with other tools.

Contact

Open an issue on the Tacket GitHub repository for general privacy questions. For security vulnerabilities, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting when available, or open a minimal public issue asking for a maintainer security contact without including private details.
