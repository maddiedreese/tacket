Private AI chat library

Save the AI chats you want to use again.

Tacket saves ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini conversations on your Mac, makes them searchable, and lets you copy or send the full conversation to Codex, Claude Code, or your clipboard when you need the context again.

Download for Mac View source

Free forever. Open source forever. Your chats stay on your machine: no accounts, no analytics, and I never see what you save.

1

Open a chat

Go to the ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini conversation you want to save.

2

Click save

Click the extension and Tacket saves that conversation on your Mac.

3

Find it later

Browse, search, and send the full conversation wherever you need it next.

No summary step

Useful context often lives in the details. Tacket saves the conversation itself, including code blocks and message order, so you do not have to ask another model to summarize it and hope the important parts survive.

Local by default

Saved chats are local files on your Mac. There is no Tacket backend, no hosted database, no telemetry, and no model/API calls.

Store install, local chats

The Chrome extension may be installed through the Chrome Web Store, but your saved chat text goes only to the Tacket app on your Mac. It is not sent to me or to a Tacket server.
