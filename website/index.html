Local thread handoff for AI chats

Move one chat thread into another tool.

Tacket lets you capture an individual ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini thread and transfer the full conversation into Codex, Claude Code, or your clipboard with the click of a button.

Download for Mac View source

It will always be free. It will always be open source. It runs fully locally on your machine: no accounts, no analytics, and I do not ever see any of your chats.

1

Open a thread

Use Chrome on a supported ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini conversation.

2

Click capture

Tacket saves that one thread locally as a readable bundle.

3

Transfer it

Send the raw conversation to Codex, Claude Code, or the clipboard.

Built for handoff, not summaries

The main use case is moving the exact context from one chat session into another tool. Tacket keeps the thread intact instead of asking an AI model to compress it first.

Local by default

Captures are written to your Mac as inspectable files. There is no Tacket backend, no hosted database, no telemetry, and no model/API calls.
