Local transcript library

Keep your AI chats searchable on your Mac.

Save ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini threads as local .tacket bundles, search the raw transcript text later, and send the exact conversation to Codex, Claude Code, or your clipboard when you need it. No summary step.

Free forever. Open source forever. Fully local on your machine: no accounts, no analytics, and I never see your chats.

1

Open a thread

Go to the ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini conversation you want to keep.

2

Click capture

Click the extension and Tacket saves that exact thread to your local library.

3

Search or transfer

Find saved context later, then paste or launch the full raw transcript where it belongs.

Built for raw transcripts

Sometimes the useful context is already in a chat. Tacket keeps the thread as-is, with code blocks and message order intact, so your saved library and transfers are based on the conversation itself.

Local by default

Captures are plain local files on your Mac. There is no Tacket backend, no hosted database, no telemetry, and no model/API calls.

Store install, local data

The Chrome extension may be installed through the Chrome Web Store, but captured chat text is sent only to the Tacket app on your Mac through Chrome Native Messaging. It is not sent to me or to a Tacket server.
